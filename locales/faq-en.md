# Supplier Carbon Inventory Survey FAQ

> This FAQ addresses common questions about completing ANYKING INTERNATIONAL TRADING's
> *Supplier Carbon Inventory & Management Survey v4.0*. It covers all scenarios — from
> suppliers that have never conducted a carbon inventory to those already certified
> under ISO 14064-1 / ISO 14067 / SBTi.

---

## General: Overall questions before filling out {#general}

### Q1. Our company has never conducted a carbon inventory. Do we really need to complete this survey? {#q1}

**Yes.** Section 4 (Path Routing) of the survey defines four paths, and **"Neither implemented nor planned" is a valid option (Path ④)** — not a reason for rejection.

ANYKING wants a complete picture of supply chain status; data from "blank-slate" suppliers is equally valuable, as it determines who gets prioritized for group coaching and 1-on-1 consultation resources. The real risk is not filling it out, or delaying.

---

### Q2. Will completing this survey expose our cost structure or trade secrets to ANYKING? {#q2}

The survey states that data is "for internal supply chain management reference only, not for external disclosure." Three layers of protection apply in practice:

- Confidential information in supporting attachments (inventories, reports) **can be redacted**
- Section 7 (Supplementary Information) has a dedicated consent clause (F+5), allowing options such as "Subject to NDA" or "Only publicly disclosed information"
- Procurement share questions (E-7, F-4) use **range selection**, not exact figures

---

### Q3. Does it really only take 15–20 minutes to complete? {#q3}

**Only for mature suppliers on Path ① (SBTi-validated) or Path ② (ISO 14064-1 completed).**

For Path ④ (blank slate), thoroughly completing Section 7 typically takes 30–60 minutes and may require cross-departmental input (facilities for electricity consumption, procurement for raw material usage).

> Recommendation: spend 5 minutes scanning the entire survey first, identify who to consult, then complete section by section.

---

### Q4. Which questions are mandatory and which can be skipped? {#q4}

Questions marked with "***Mandatory***" are required. **The most important decision point is D-1 Path Routing** — whichever path you select determines which section is your primary focus; other sections become optional or exempt.

> Recommendation: complete D-1 first, then follow its skip-logic guidance.

---

### Q5. Our company qualifies under multiple statuses (e.g., we have ISO 14064-1 and ISO 14067 for some SKUs). How should we mark D-1? {#q5}

The survey instructions clearly state: "**Please select the most comprehensive status and complete all relevant sections.**"

Recommendation: select Path ③ (ISO 14067), and also complete Section 5 truthfully — this is a positive signal, demonstrating disclosure capability at both organizational and product levels.

---

### Q6. What will ANYKING do with our data after we submit? What happens next? {#q6}

Per the survey, the data will be used for five purposes:

1. Reporting to end-brand customers
2. Planning coaching resources
3. Negotiating preferential rates for verification
4. Matching 1-on-1 consultation
5. Inclusion in the "Indicator Supplier" program

> **"Indicator Supplier" is the keyword** — it represents priority consideration for future orders and new projects.

## Sections 1–2: Basic information questions {#basic-info}

### Q7. Does B-3 "Company Size" refer to a single entity or group consolidation? {#q7}

It refers to **the legal entity completing this survey**. If you are a group subsidiary, report the subsidiary's employee count; the parent company is disclosed separately in B-5.

---

### Q8. B-5 asks if we are a group member. What does "group-level verification" mean? {#q8}

It refers to whether the parent company or group conducts a **consolidated ISO 14064-1 verification** that covers your subsidiary.

If yes, you may reference the group certificate in Section 5 — no separate verification needed for the subsidiary. If uncertain, contact your group's sustainability or finance department.

---

### Q9. Are the site codes "P1, P2" in B-6 specified by ANYKING, or do we define them? {#q9}

**You define them**; P1, P2 are merely format examples.

Recommendation: use meaningful codes (e.g., TW-TN, VN-HCM). These codes are referenced in Section 3 (C-1) "Production Site" and Section 5 (E-2) "Organizational Boundary" — consistency prevents confusion.

---

### Q10. We have 5 sites supplying ANYKING, but the table only has 3 rows. How do we handle this? {#q10}

Per the survey instructions: "**If there are more than 3 sites, please attach a supplementary list.**"

Recommendation: provide a complete Excel list, ensuring filename and site codes match this survey.

---

### Q11. We don't have a dedicated carbon inventory liaison (B-7). Who should fill this in? {#q11}

Per the survey: "**Please assign a manager or designated person who can access all information requested in this survey.**" In practice:

- **SMEs**: plant manager, QA manager, GM assistant
- **Larger enterprises**: ESG/CSR department, EHS department, QA director

The key requirement: this person must be able to coordinate cross-departmental responses to follow-up inquiries from ANYKING or consultants.

## Section 3: Product / raw material inventory questions {#product-list}

### Q12. C-1 says "one row per independent SKU," but we supply 50 SKUs to ANYKING. Do we list all of them? {#q12}

Generally yes. However, if SKU count is excessive:

- **Same product, multiple specs but shared carbon footprint** — merge into one row, list all specs in the "Specification" column (explicitly allowed by the survey)
- **Genuinely 50+ distinct products** — list the top 5 in the table and provide an Excel appendix for the rest
- **Confirm the "key SKU" list with ANYKING procurement** to focus your reporting

---

### Q13. For C-1 "Primary Material," what if the product is a composite (e.g., stainless steel + plastic)? {#q13}

Recommendation: use a "**primary + secondary**" format, e.g., "304 stainless steel (85%) + ABS (15%)."

If the weight ratio is uncertain, "primarily stainless steel, with plastic components" is acceptable. **This column is mainly used to estimate upstream carbon hotspots — it does not require a precise BOM breakdown.**

---

### Q14. For C-1 "2025 Volume Supplied to ANYKING," we haven't closed the books yet. Can we use estimates? {#q14}

Yes. Recommendation: estimate as "**delivered volume + ordered but not yet delivered volume**," and note in the remarks "estimate as of YYYY/MM/DD."

ANYKING will not use this figure for settlement — it is solely for estimating procurement share and carbon emission allocation.

---

### Q15. For C-1 "Carbon Footprint Report," we have PCR certification but not ISO 14067. Does that count as "Yes"? {#q15}

**Yes, mark "Yes,"** but in Section 6 F-2 "Applicable Standard," honestly select the actual standard used (e.g., PAS 2050, GHG Protocol Product Standard).

ANYKING will evaluate holistically and will not reject responses simply because they are not ISO 14067.

---

## Section 4: Path routing questions {#path-routing}

### Q16. What is the difference between SBTi "Validated" and "Committed but not yet validated"? Which one qualifies for D-1 Path ①? {#q16}

| Status | Definition | D-1 Path |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Validated** | Target has passed SBTi official review and is listed on the dashboard (URL contains "Validated") | Path ① |
| **Committed** | Only the Commitment Letter submitted; target not yet under review | **Cannot select Path ①**; select Path ②③④ based on inventory status |

The distinction matters: Validated allows exemption from ISO 14064-1 / 14067 disclosure; Committed does not.

---

### Q17. We've conducted ISO 14064-1 but not yet verified. Which path should we select in D-1? {#q17}

Refer to E-1 status:

- "Inventory completed in the past 2 years, **verification not yet scheduled**" (E-1 option B) → Still Path ②, but specify expected verification timing in I-2
- Path ② literally reads "**Has provided / Will provide**" — the word "Will" covers inventoried-but-not-yet-verified cases

---

### Q18. We have both ISO 14064-1 and ISO 14067, but only one SKU has 14067. How do we select? {#q18}

Recommendation: select **Path ③**, then:

- Complete Section 5 (ISO 14064-1) truthfully (optional but beneficial for your profile)
- In Section 6 (ISO 14067), report **only the SKU with 14067**; mark other SKUs as D or E in F-1
- For SKUs without 14067, proceed to Section 7 (Supplementary Information)

---

## Section 5: ISO 14064-1 organizational inventory questions {#iso14064}

### Q19. Our inventory was conducted in 2022. Does that count as "within the past 2 years"? {#q19}

**No.** The survey's "past 2 years" is relative to the survey completion date (2026), so a 2022 inventory falls under E-1 option D: "Previously conducted (more than 2 years ago), no longer maintained."

This is not a negative label — it simply triggers the explanation requirement in E-5.

---

### Q20. In E-2 "Organizational Boundary," we want to select "Single site," but we actually have a company-level inventory (covering HQ + plant). How should we fill this in? {#q20}

Select "**Multi-site consolidated**" to reflect reality, and list all site codes (including HQ) in the corresponding field.

If HQ doesn't have a code in B-6, add a code like "HQ" or "OFFICE" and note it accordingly.

---

### Q21. For E-3 "Scope 3 (if any)," we calculated upstream raw materials but only Category 1. Should we report it? {#q21}

**Yes, and specify the category.** Recommended format: "Cat 1: X tCO2e (upstream raw materials only)."

Full Scope 3 coverage is rare; partial-category disclosure is more valuable than leaving the field blank — ANYKING's customers, when subject to SBTi, typically require disclosure of material Scope 3 categories rather than full-category coverage.

---

### Q22. In E-4 "Verification Body Accreditation Country / Authority," what do TAF, CNAS, ANAB mean? {#q22}

These are **accreditation bodies** that confer international recognition on verification bodies:

| Abbreviation | Full Name | Country / Region |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **TAF** | Taiwan Accreditation Foundation | Taiwan (used by TÜV, SGS, BSI Taiwan branches) |
| **CNAS** | China National Accreditation Service | China |
| **ANAB** | ANSI National Accreditation Board | United States |
| **UKAS** | United Kingdom Accreditation Service | United Kingdom |

> Accreditation logos are printed on certificates — check the first page of the certificate PDF.

---

### Q23. Why does E-7 ask for procurement share in three tiers (weight / value / quantity)? What if we only have value data? {#q23}

This is because the **scientific hierarchy for carbon allocation is: weight > value > quantity.**

- Weight share best reflects actual emission responsibility
- Value is easily distorted by market price fluctuations

If you only have value data, fill in the range under the value tier and mark the other two as "Not available / Not applicable." **This is fully acceptable** — the survey explicitly designates this as a priority order.

---

## Section 6: ISO 14067 product carbon footprint questions {#iso14067}

### Q24. What is a "Functional Unit" in F-2? If our product is a 1 kg chemical, is the FU simply 1 kg? {#q24}

**Usually yes, but PCR rules govern.** A Functional Unit (FU) is "the quantified reference describing product function." Common mappings:

| Product Type | Common FU |
| --- | --- |
| Bulk chemicals / raw materials | 1 kg, 1 tonne |
| Packaged food | 1 pack (net weight), 1 litre |
| Textile fabric | 1 m², 1 kg |
| Electronic components | 1 piece, 1 unit |

If a PCR (Product Category Rule) exists, follow its FU; otherwise use the procurement unit for clarity.

> **The key is FU alignment with ANYKING's procurement unit.** If misaligned, F-2 includes a "Conversion Factor" field.

---

### Q25. For F-2 "Verification Boundary," how do we choose between Cradle-to-Gate and Cradle-to-Grave? {#q25}

| Boundary | Scope | Applicable to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Cradle-to-Gate** | From raw material extraction to product leaving your factory gate | **Most common for B2B intermediate suppliers**, since you don't know how downstream uses or disposes of the product |
| **Cradle-to-Grave** | Adds transport, use, and end-of-life stages | **Typically only for final products** (e.g., phones, vehicles) |

As a supplier, you are most likely Cradle-to-Gate — don't be misled by the "Grave is more complete" wording.

---

### Q26. For F-2 "Database used," we use ecoinvent built into SimaPro, but we don't know which version. How do we find it? {#q26}

Open SimaPro → Help → About, or click any process → check the Database field. It will show something like `ecoinvent 3.X cutoff`.

**Version number matters**, because emission factors differ between ecoinvent 3.8, 3.9, 3.10, affecting data comparability.

---

### Q27. For F-2 "Report / Certificate Validity Management," which option is best to select? {#q27}

It's not about "best" — it's about **reflecting actual practice**. Each option has its meaning:

- **Annual update** — high cost, but customers value it most
- **Per certificate validity (usually 3 years)** — mainstream practice
- **Material change threshold (≥5%)** — internal control mechanism in mature enterprises

If you've never thought about this, selecting "Other" and writing "**No fixed update mechanism currently; planned to establish by YYYY**" is an honest answer.

---

### Q28. Both F-4 and E-7 ask about procurement share. What's the difference? {#q28}

| Question | Denominator | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **E-7** | ANYKING's procurement as a share of **the entire company** | Scope 3 allocation in organizational inventory |
| **F-4** | ANYKING's procurement as a share of **annual production of that SKU** | Allocation in product carbon footprint |

Both must be completed — each serves a different allocation purpose.

---

## Section 7: Supplementary information (activity data) questions {#supplementary}

### Q29. Section 7 is marked "optional." Can we really skip it? {#q29}

**Legally yes; practically, strongly recommended to complete.** Three reasons:

1. F+5 includes a dedicated confidentiality clause — data will not be forwarded to third parties
2. These activity data (electricity, materials) are **ready-made building blocks** for future carbon inventory — completing them is an investment in your own carbon assets
3. ANYKING considers Section 7 completion as a significant factor when evaluating "Indicator Supplier" priority

---

### Q30. For F+1 "Annual Electricity Consumption," we have multiple sites. Which one do we report? {#q30}

Per the survey: "**Report the site primarily supplying our company**" — i.e., the site marked "Yes" in B-6's "Primary supplier to ANYKING" column.

If multiple sites qualify as primary suppliers, duplicate this question for each, or report a consolidated figure with a note specifying covered sites.

---

### Q31. How do we calculate renewable energy share in F+1? Can we combine on-site solar, purchased green power, and Renewable Energy Certificates (RECs)? {#q31}

**Yes** — the survey explicitly states "including purchased green power, on-site solar, and RECs." Formula:

```
(On-site renewable generation + Purchased green power + Equivalent kWh from held RECs) ÷ Total electricity consumption × 100%
```

> Note: RECs must correspond to **that year's** electricity consumption — no double counting.

---

### Q32. How is the "weight share" calculated for the top 3 raw materials in F+2? Is it relative to finished product or input materials? {#q32}

Recommendation: use **share of total input weight** (including process losses), since inventory accounting focuses on "how much raw material entered the factory" rather than "how much finished product left."

If your product has a defined BOM, the BOM weight ratio is also acceptable — **just note the calculation basis in the remarks**.

---

### Q33. For F+4 "Refrigerant Leakage," we only use R410A in air conditioners. Does that count? {#q33}

**Yes, but apply a threshold judgment.** Refrigerant leakage from standard office air conditioners is typically minimal (absent service events), but ISO 14064-1 still requires disclosure. Recommendation:

- **If you have refrigeration / cooling processes** (food, pharma, chemical) → Mark "Yes" and elaborate in F+1
- **General office AC only, no refrigerant top-ups recorded** → Mark "None" with a note: "Standard AC with R410A only, no annual top-up records"

---

## Sections 8–9: Reduction targets and measures questions {#targets-actions}

### Q34. For G-1 "Reduction Target," we have an energy-saving plan but no formal reduction target. What should we select? {#q34}

Select "**Not yet set**" or "**In planning**."

An energy-saving plan is a **measure** (Section 9, H-1); a reduction target is a **commitment figure against a baseline year** (e.g., 30% reduction by 2030 vs. 2020) — they are different.

Honest selection is not penalized — in fact, it allows I-3 group coaching to be precisely matched to your needs.

---

### Q35. G-4 says ANYKING expects ≥30% reduction by 2032 (annualized ~3%). We haven't even set a baseline year. How should we respond? {#q35}

Recommendation: select "**Willing to align, requires external support**."

This option was designed precisely for this scenario — ANYKING will include you in subsequent group coaching, and a consultant will assist with baseline setting and reduction path estimation.

> **Selecting "Unable to align" requires stating a reason and will lower your Indicator Supplier rating — choose carefully.**

---

### Q36. For H-1 matrix "Estimated Reduction," we genuinely don't know how to calculate. Can we leave it blank? {#q36}

- **Execution status and start year** — these two columns are essential; must complete
- **Primary action** — brief description suffices (e.g., "Facility-wide LED replacement")
- **Estimated reduction** — you can **select a range** (the survey explicitly states "ranges may also be selected"), e.g., "approx. 5–10%" or "approx. 50–100 tCO2e/year"; if truly unknown, write "to be quantified"

> **Leaving fields entirely blank will be interpreted as "no such measure."** If you've done it, report it — don't abandon fields out of fear of inaccuracy.

---

### Q37. For H-2 "Emission Hotspots," we don't know our largest emission source. How do we select? {#q37}

This question is specifically designed to **provide rough ranking for companies that have not yet inventoried**. Intuitive judgment is sufficient:

| Industry Type | Common Hotspots |
| --- | --- |
| Steel, cement, glass, chemical | Raw material emissions + process emissions |
| Electronic assembly, textile, plastics | Purchased electricity dominant |
| Food processing | Purchased electricity + gas boilers |
| Logistics | Upstream/downstream transport |

Don't worry about misselection — this is preliminary screening; subsequent inventory will correct it.

## Section 10: Cooperation willingness and coaching needs questions {#cooperation}

### Q38. Will selecting "Limited cooperation" in I-1 be interpreted by ANYKING as non-cooperation? {#q38}

**No**, but explain the limits in I-6. Common reasonable limitations include:

- "**Confidential data requires NDA**"
- "**Group-level unified channel may slow response time**"
- "**Can only provide content already disclosed in our sustainability report**"

> Honest explanation + reasonable limits > full cooperation that fails to deliver.

---

### Q39. Can we change our mind after signing up for the "Carbon Inventory Bootcamp" in I-3? What is the discount level? {#q39}

The survey is an "**intent survey**" — selecting "Yes, interested" is not a binding registration. C.C.Sustain (the consulting partner) will subsequently send detailed proposals, pricing, and schedule for you to decide.

If you want to evaluate before deciding, "Need detailed information and quotation first" is the safest option.

---

### Q40. I-5 asks whether we find our own verification body or have ANYKING facilitate. What's the difference? {#q40}

| Option | When applicable | Advantage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Self-arranged** | You have an existing verification partner, or specific requirements (e.g., must be TAF-accredited) | Full freedom |
| **ANYKING-facilitated** | SMEs without an existing partner | ANYKING uses aggregate supplier volume for **collective negotiation**, typically 15–30% rate discount, but the verification body is ANYKING-recommended |
| **Undecided** | Want to keep options open | Decide later based on terms |

> For SMEs without existing partnerships, "ANYKING-facilitated" generally offers better value.

## Sections 11–12: Attachments and declaration questions {#attachments}

### Q41. We can't obtain the ISO 14064-1 certificate at the moment (group is still consolidating). How do we fill in the attachment checklist? {#q41}

**Mark the "Expected submission date" column with a specific date** (e.g., 2026/08/31).

This is better than leaving it blank — it demonstrates control over the timeline. If even the expected date is uncertain, write "Pending group confirmation" and explain group verification progress in I-6.

---

### Q42. To what extent can we redact the "ISO 14064-1 Emissions Inventory Summary (confidentiality may be redacted)"? {#q42}

Recommendation: retain the following fields:

- Emission source category (Scope 1/2/3 + source name)
- Emission quantity (tCO2e)
- Calculation method (activity data × emission factor)
- Emission factor source (IPCC, Taiwan grid emission factor, etc.)

**Can be redacted**: specific activity data (e.g., actual kWh consumed), equipment count, unit cost information.

> The goal is for ANYKING to understand the "emission source structure" — not to see every detail.

---

### Q43. Does Section 12 "Company Seal / Signature (if required)" need an official seal? {#q43}

Depends on your company's internal control policy. In practice:

- **Routine correspondence** — signature of the filler suffices
- **Commitments involved** (e.g., I-1, I-4) — recommend departmental or company seal
- **If ANYKING explicitly requests** — follow ANYKING's specific notice

---

### Q44. What is the follow-up process after submission? {#q44}

Reasonable inference from the survey content:

1. ANYKING internal consolidation → classification into four paths → rating
2. **Path ①②③ suppliers**: verify attachment completeness, include in annual supply chain carbon management report
3. **Path ④ suppliers**: assign 1-on-1 channel, evaluate inclusion in group coaching
4. **"Indicator Supplier" list** announced at a designated time, with priority access to new project opportunities

> Recommendation: add to I-6 "**Request feedback on rating results**" — even if ANYKING doesn't proactively share, they will typically respond when requested.
